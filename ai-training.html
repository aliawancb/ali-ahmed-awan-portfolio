Where the examples come from

AI I've put into production

The four AI products are FastRead.io, Myautobio, Clintwin/Medsim AI and Formulai. The notes below are the ones already published on the site.

FastRead.io

Helps people write a book with AI

Paid conversion moved from 3% to 11% in one quarter. Across the whole portfolio, AI running costs came down 38%, with better output, not worse.

Myautobio

Completed life stories

Taken from idea to a live product in under 90 days, with FastRead.io, Clintwin/Medsim AI and Formulai.

Clintwin / Medsim AI

Virtual patients for training medical students

Not a chatbot with a medical skin on it. The design brief was about what happens when a model is convincing but wrong.

Formulai

Personalized health profiles

One of the four AI products taken from idea to market in under 90 days.

AI resume parser

Messy resumes, including handwritten ones

Turns them into compliant CVs in under 15 minutes instead of hours. Built as part of the Tribal Health work, after a move off legacy software.

In the workshops, teams consistently see a bigger accuracy jump from fixing chunking and adding a re-ranking step than from upgrading the model. That is written up in RAG isn't a model problem, it's a retrieval problem.
