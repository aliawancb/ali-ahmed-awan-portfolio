How I work

Find the real problem. Prove the fix fast. Build a team that keeps going.

I started out as a business analyst in Lahore, turning customer conversations into wireframes. The products got bigger (portfolios, new countries, production AI), but the instinct hasn't changed. These four ideas guide every engagement.

Product first, always

Continuous discovery, a clear roadmap and fast validation. Every sprint should trace back to something a real user needs.

Where AI earns its keep

I build with the models myself, prototyping alongside engineers to cut validation time and running costs.

Growth through insight

Data tells you what happened; users tell you why. I pair the two to move acquisition, retention and revenue.

Teams that ship

Crisp goals, honest reviews and a culture where design, engineering and business pull in the same direction.

Product consulting

When a big decision is on the table (a new product, a pricing change, a new market) and you want it pressure-tested.

03

AI transformation

When you know AI matters, but not yet where it pays off, or how to use it safely.

  • Finding the use cases worth building
  • Choosing models, connecting them to your data, keeping costs in check
  • Hands-on training, so your team actually uses it
In practice 38% lower AI running costs across a product portfolio, with better output, not worse.
04

Agile & process

When the team is busy, but not shipping what matters most.

  • Clear rules for discovery, prioritization and specs
  • Smooth handoffs from design to QA to release
